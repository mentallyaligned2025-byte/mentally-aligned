Why do I overthink everything?

Overthinking is a mind trying to protect you. It scans for danger, replays mistakes and rehearses futures so nothing catches you off guard.

Thinking about a problem produces answers. Overthinking circles the same facts and produces only tiredness. The shift from one to the other is usually quiet: you stop learning and start looping.

Three ways to stop overthinking today

  • A worry window. Pick 15 minutes earlier in the day to worry on purpose, with a timer. When a worry shows up outside that window, tell it “not now, it’s scheduled.” This technique comes from cognitive behavioral therapy.
  • A brain dump. Write every thought on paper for two minutes without editing. A thought on paper is easier to look at than one circling in your head.
  • Put the thought on trial. Write the thought, the evidence for it, the evidence against it, and a more balanced version. Therapists call this cognitive restructuring.

How to stop overthinking at night

At night the distractions fall away and the mind gets loud. Do your worry window earlier in the day, keep a notepad by the bed, and if you’re still awake after about 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet until you feel sleepy.

When overthinking is more than a habit

If overthinking is constant, disrupts your sleep or daily life, or comes with ongoing anxiety or low mood, a licensed therapist or doctor can help. This page is a self-help tool, not treatment.

Questions people ask

How do I stop overthinking everything?

Start by sorting the thought: are you replaying the past, worrying about the future, guessing what someone thinks, or stuck on a decision? Each loop needs a different exit. Act on the part you can, then schedule the rest for a short worry window. The free tool on this page walks you through it in two minutes.

Why do I overthink so much?

Often because your mind is trying to feel safe or certain. Stress, tiredness, perfectionism and uncertainty make it louder. Overthinking is a common habit, and habits can be retrained.

How do I stop overthinking at night?

Move your worrying earlier in the day with a worry window, write racing thoughts on paper, and if you are still awake after about 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet until you feel sleepy.

Is overthinking a mental health problem?

Overthinking on its own is common and is not a diagnosis. If it is constant, hard to control, or comes with ongoing anxiety or low mood, it is worth talking to a licensed professional.
